Refund Policy

Crisp · built in Grand Rapids · published 2026-07-28 · last updated 2026-11-11
Quick answer

If Crisp does not work for you, email us within 30 days of purchase and we will refund you. You do not need to give a reason.

Crisp is published by Kerr & Company LLC, a Michigan limited liability company. Payments are processed by a merchant of record, which is the seller on your receipt and the party that issues the refund. Since 20 August 2026 that is Dodo Payments; purchases made before then went through Polar, and a Polar receipt is still refunded by Polar. Your receipt names whichever one you bought from.

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

How to request a refund

Email support@crispvideo.app from the address you purchased with, or use your receipt email. Include your licence key if you have it. Refunds are issued to the original payment method through whichever merchant of record sold you the licence (Dodo Payments for purchases from 20 August 2026, Polar before that) and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. You do not need to work out which one: email us and we will action it.

Try before you buy

Crisp is free to use without a licence. The free tier runs the full editor and the standard enhancement lanes; video exports carry a small "crispvideo.app" watermark. We would rather you confirm Crisp does what you need before paying than rely on a refund afterwards.

UK and EU customers

You normally have a 14-day right to cancel a purchase of digital content. Because Crisp is supplied immediately on purchase, you are asked to acknowledge at checkout that supply begins right away and that you therefore lose that statutory cancellation right once it does. Our 30-day policy above is offered voluntarily and is more generous than the statutory minimum, so in practice you are not worse off.

After a refund

We would rather be straight with you about what happens on the computer you already activated. A Pro copy re-checks its licence about once a week when the computer is online, and a refunded key turns Pro off at the next check. Offline, or when the check cannot reach the licence server, nothing changes until it can; a check never uses up an activation. Once Pro is off, the free tier keeps the whole editor and every standard enhancement lane, and video exports carry a small watermark. Files you already exported remain yours to keep and use.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback. We will almost certainly just refund you, and it is faster for both of us.
