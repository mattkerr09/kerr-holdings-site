The commission

50% of each sale made with your code, calculated on what the buyer paid for the licence before any sales tax. After your tenth sale the rate rises to 60%, for the eleventh sale and every sale after it. A sale that is refunded earns nothing. A sale made without your code cannot be credited to you, because the code is the only record that the sale was yours.

When it is paid

Commission on a sale becomes payable 30 days after the sale. That is the refund window for Crisp, Docket SEO and AdPlaybook (Outlier’s is 14 days), and holding every sale for the full 30 days means commission is never paid on a sale that is then refunded. Whatever has become payable is paid once a month, by bank transfer.

How tracking works

You get a personal code. The buyer types it into the discount-code box at checkout, and the sale is recorded against that code at the moment of purchase. There is no tracking link, no cookie and no attribution window, so nothing depends on the buyer’s browser — and nothing is credited unless the code is used. Put it wherever you mention the app: in the description, on screen, in the text, beside the link.

How to apply

Email hello@kerrandcompanyholdings.com and say where you would promote — the site, channel, newsletter or community, with a link — and which app fits the people who read or watch you. A person reads every application and replies by email. The programme is new and small; it is being opened deliberately, a few affiliates at a time.

What to know before you apply

Mac only, and Apple Silicon only. All four run on a Mac with an Apple Silicon chip and on nothing else: there is no Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad or Intel Mac version. If your audience is not on that hardware the code will not convert, and it is better to say so here.

No earnings claims. This page publishes no figure for what affiliates earn, because the programme has no history to report. What you earn is 50%, then 60%, of the sales you send, and nothing on this page implies more.

Say that you are paid. Wherever you use the code, disclose that you earn a commission on sales. In the United States the FTC’s endorsement guides require that disclosure; this programme requires it everywhere.

The product documents control. Prices, refund terms and system requirements are stated on each product’s own site and in its terms, linked from the entity page. If this page and those documents ever differ, the documents are the ones that apply.
