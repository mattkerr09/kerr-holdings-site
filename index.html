How purchases are processed

Software purchases are handled by Polar as merchant of record. Polar takes the payment, is responsible for sales tax and VAT where it applies, and issues the licence key by email. Your receipt comes from Polar, and a refund request may be sent either to Polar or to the address above.

Refund windows differ by product and are stated on each product’s own refunds page, linked above. Those pages are authoritative; this one deliberately does not restate the terms, because a summary that drifts from the document it summarises is worse than no summary.

A note on the software

These products use machine-learning models that produce plausible output rather than verified fact. Crisp reconstructs detail that was not in the source. AdPlaybook drafts copy you are responsible for before it runs. Docket reports what it could and says so when it could not see something. Outlier runs models locally and inherits their limitations.

Review the output before you rely on it, and read the limitations in each product’s terms. Where a product states that it cannot check something, that statement is the honest one and is meant to be read.
